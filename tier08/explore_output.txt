5. Noise: depolarizing error probability p on every CX
          p   Shor: peak hits   Shor: 8 shots give factors   Grover: success
     0.0000          96.7%                    99.2%            97.8%
     0.0001          81.7%                    99.6%            83.0%
     0.0003          50.0%                    97.0%            65.0%
     0.0010          13.3%                    64.2%            31.0%
   A random bit string lands on a peak 2.7% of the time, so Shor's peak hits fall to
   about that when it is scrambled. Today's hardware is around p = 0.001 and these
   circuits have thousands of CX gates: neither would survive without error correction.
   Zero-noise extrapolation and error cancellation could stretch this a little, but
   with ~5000 CX gates the signal is gone long before extrapolation can recover it.
